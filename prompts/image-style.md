# LumosLens – Image Style Prompt (v1, approved)

Image prompts are written in **English** because image models follow English better. Narration, subtitles and quiz stay in Vietnamese.

> Source of truth: `IMAGE_STYLE_BASE` and `IMAGE_NEGATIVE` in [server/src/prompts.ts](../server/src/prompts.ts).

Each scene image = `STYLE_BASE` + `CHARACTER_SHEET` (per story) + `SCENE_PROMPT` (per scene) + `NEGATIVE`.

---

## 1. STYLE_BASE (fixed for every image)

```
Children's science picture-book illustration, soft 2.5D digital painting,
gentle rounded shapes, clean confident linework, friendly and warm mood.
Bright natural daylight, soft shadows, airy light background with plenty of
negative space. Palette: warm cream (#FCF9F2, #F6EBD7) and soft butter yellow
(#FFF1C9), accents of brand blue (#1E419D), deep navy (#0F2452) and sunlight
yellow (#FFC93C). Preserve the natural colors of the depicted objects and
phenomena. Low-to-medium detail, one clear focal subject,
simple uncluttered background. Scientifically accurate depiction of objects
and phenomena, shown in a way a 7–12 year old can understand.
Vertical 9:16 portrait composition. Main subject placed in the central
upper two-thirds; keep the bottom 30% calm and low-detail (subtitle area).
No text anywhere in the image.
```

## 2. CHARACTER_SHEET (generated by the Story Planner, once per story)

The planner returns a short, fixed description of each recurring character or object. It is appended to **every** scene prompt so the scenes stay consistent. Example:

```
Recurring characters: Isaac Newton as a young man in his early 20s,
friendly stylized face, shoulder-length wavy brown hair, simple dark
17th-century coat with white collar. Recurring object: a single round
red apple with one green leaf.
```

Rules:
- Historical figures: stylized and friendly, in period-accurate clothing. No realistic portraits.
- Never redraw a person who appears in the user's photo.
- If the story has no characters (a step-by-step phenomenon), describe only the recurring object or phenomenon.

## 3. SCENE_PROMPT (per scene, generated by the Planner)

Template: `[Shot: wide | medium | close-up] of [subject] [action/phenomenon], [setting], [scientific detail that must be visible].`

Example: `Medium shot of young Newton sitting under an apple tree in an English orchard, looking up thoughtfully as an apple falls straight down; a faint dotted arc hints at the pull of gravity toward the ground.`

- Each scene has **one** main visual idea that matches its `key_fact`.
- Arrows, dotted lines and enlarged cutaways may illustrate the phenomenon, but **no text or numbers**.

## 4. NEGATIVE (fixed)

```
Avoid: text, letters, numbers, captions, labels, logos, watermarks, signatures,
photorealism, real children's faces, scary, violent, gory or horror imagery,
weapons, unsafe experiments, neon glow, heavy gradients, lens flares,
cluttered backgrounds, distorted hands or extra limbs.
```

## 5. Technical specs

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Aspect ratio | 9:16 (requested via `imageConfig.aspectRatio`) |
| Delivered to the app | JPEG, resized to max 1080 px wide, quality 82 (sharp/mozjpeg) |
| Prelude teaser | Currently uses the user's own photo (no extra image generation) |
| Fallback when an image is late or fails | The user's photo with a slow Ken Burns pan; if there is no photo, a light cream #F9F2E5 → #F6EBD7 background with an icon. Never an empty frame |
| Free-tier note | Image models have a quota of 0 on the free tier; billing must be enabled for AI images |
